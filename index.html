FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions parents often ask.

What grades does Adera Learning support?

Adera currently focuses on students in grades 3–8.

What subjects do you help with?

Our primary focus is math and homework support, with additional help in academic skills, organization, study habits, and related schoolwork.

How are students placed into groups?

We use the initial assessment and academic needs to place students with an appropriate group. Skill level matters more than simply matching grade numbers.

What happens during the free assessment?

The 20–30 minute assessment helps us understand the student's current skills, learning needs, confidence, and priorities so we can recommend the right support.

Will parents receive updates?

Yes. Parent communication is a core part of Adera. Group programs include a monthly progress update so families understand what their child is working on and where growth is happening.
